# Pathway

**Always know what to do next in World of Warcraft: Forever.** Pathway knows every quest your character can do, picks what's worth doing next, and gets you there the fastest way there is: on foot, by flight path, boat, zeppelin, tram, hearthstone or teleport. It also handles the things that slow you down: quest dialogs, rewards, gear upgrades, bags, finding a repair or a trainer, and knowing when to move on. Every part can be switched off.

## Questing

**Three ways to quest, your pick** (in setup, at the top of the guide, in the journal or the map menu)
- **Fastest XP** (the default): only the highest-XP quests at your level (the top 25%). It uses what each quest really pays on Forever. You get a set (the top quests in one zone), pick it up, finish it and hand it in without picking up anything else. The next set is wherever gives the most XP per minute: a big set a flight or a boat away beats a lone quest next door, on either continent, and the card shows where it is.
- **Storyline**: the story chain that fits your level best, start to finish, then the next best one.
- **All quests**: every quest in the zone, one chain at a time, with quests on their own picked up as you pass them.
- On Storyline and All quests, Pathway tells you where to set your hearthstone when most of your hand-ins sit around one inn, and adds it as a step when you pass it.

**Go anywhere**: the globe button on the minimap bar (or `/pw to westfall`). Type a zone, city or town (Gadgetzan, Booty Bay, Menethil Harbor...), see the fastest way there leg by leg with times, and go. It weighs the roads and passes, your flight paths, boats, zeppelins, the tram, the Feathermoon ferry, your hearthstone, mage and druid teleports, Astral Recall and the engineering transporters. On the flight map, an arrow points at the flight point to take.

**Minimap buttons**: Journal, Next set, Find, Go anywhere, Outfits (when you have saved outfits) and Pathway sit in the minimap's button bar with the calendar (or around Blizzard's minimap, where you can drag them). Other add-ons' buttons only go on the bar if you pick them (setup asks, and asks again once for each add-on you add later); the rest wait in the numbered add-on button. The notification bell sits on the left of the minimap, under the holiday button, once Pathway has something to tell you; a number on it counts what you haven't seen.

**Quest journal** (`/pw journal`, the Journal minimap button, or the guide window)
- Every quest your character can do, organized: **Zones** (by continent, with each zone's level range and how much of it you've done), **Dungeons**, **Class**, **Professions** and **Flight paths**.
- Zones that suit your level are listed first under *Good for your level*.
- Each zone shows its **chains**, in order, with progress (*3/5*), then its other quests. Filter to *Not done yet* or *Can do now*, or search every quest by name.
- Click a quest for everything about it: where it starts and ends (with coordinates), its objectives and where they drop, which step of its chain it is, what you need first, and why you can't take it yet. A 3D look at the quest giver.
- Load any of it as a guide with one click:
  - **Storyline**: the chain that fits your level, start to finish.
  - **All quests**: everything there is, chain to chain.
  - **Fastest XP**: the top quests for experience, a set at a time.
  - **Follow chain** (or right-click a chain): one chain, start to finish.
  - **Go**: a single quest.
- The step guide shows how much of the loaded guide you've done.

**On the map**
- A slim bar along the bottom: **Next set** · **Pick up** · **Objectives** · **Turn in**, for the map you're looking at.
- Pins for quests to pick up (!), to turn in (?) and where your objectives are. Click any pin to go straight there.
- Flight paths you haven't discovered glow gold on the world map, on the minimap (on its edge when they're farther away) and on Pathway's minimap. Click one to go there.
- Your route is drawn as a dotted line with numbered stops. Click a stop to do it next.

**Arrow and minimap path**
- The arrow points to your next stop, says what to do there, how far it is and about how long it takes, and turns into a ring when you arrive.
- A dotted trail on the minimap runs from you through the next few steps: bright to the next one, softer after that. It works with a rotating minimap, every zoom level, and indoors.
- **Quest spots on the minimap**: every objective spot of your quests is a bold, outlined pip in that quest's own color, with the guide's quest in your accent color, bigger and glowing. The spot the arrow wants pulses, spots you've already checked turn hollow, and when a quest's spots are all off the minimap, a chevron on the edge points the way. It's on both minimaps and can be turned off in settings.
- Crates, ore and other things you click: once you've looked at a spot, the arrow moves on to the next one, and the step says how many spots there are.
- Objectives Pathway's data can't place use the quest area the game shows on your map, so the guide can still point you there.

**Step guide window**
- A slim card: the guide and quest along the top with a progress line, then the step in plain words (*Head north-east · 230 yd*), what you're after and where it drops, and the next few steps (click one to do it next).
- The ⋯ menu: skip a step (until your next level), mark a quest completed (it never comes back on that character), the journal, stop.
- Guides flow on by themselves, the way you chose to quest: a quest into its chain, a chain into the next. When the zone is done, Pathway offers the next one.
- Quests you've already done are skipped: anything a quest in your log needed first counts as done, and when a quest giver doesn't offer a quest the guide expected, it's counted as done and the guide moves on to the next in the chain.
- Objectives update live, on the arrow and in the guide: *Defias Thug slain: 6/8*.
- An undiscovered flight path near you or your route joins the guide as a stop.

**Quest bar** (bind each box in Options > Keybindings > AddOns)
- A box for each creature this step needs, showing its face: click to target it when it's in range. Each box has its own raid marker, put on what you target so you can spot it in a crowd. Blizzard doesn't let addons target on their own, so this is one key press. The box of whatever you have targeted lights up.
- Next to them, a box for each quest item in your bags that this step uses, with its count and cooldown. Hunting bears with a wand? A box with the bear, and a box with the wand.
- Boxes can show 3D faces, still faces or plain buttons. Drag the bar by its grip, size it, or lock it.
- **Step key**: one key that targets what the step needs, then uses its item.
- **Hearthstone**: when hearthing beats running to your next stop, the bar offers your hearthstone.

**Elite quests** are tagged *Elite* with their level, in the guide and the journal. You can leave them out of suggestions.

**Level pace**
The guide window's bottom line shows your experience per hour and how long until you level. Hover it for the session: time played, quests, experience, gold and flight paths found. Each ding says how long that level took, and when a zone runs dry for you, Pathway offers the next one.

**Playing together**
Group members with Pathway see who else is on the same quest. Optionally, every quest you pick up is shared with your group. Walking into a dungeon lists its quests you have, and the ones you could have brought.

**Spotting things**
- Tooltips on mobs, NPCs and objects say what they're for.
- A ring over the nameplate of every mob your quests need.
- Optional raid marker when you mouse over one.

**Getting there fastest**
For every step Pathway weighs running (or riding), the flight paths you know, boats, zeppelins and the Deeprun Tram, your hearthstone, and teleports you know (mage teleports, Moonglade, Astral Recall), across continents. Each leg becomes a step in your guide, and the quest bar offers the hearthstone or teleport when that's the leg. Drop a map pin and Pathway takes you there, then back to where your guide left off. `/pw flights` plans a route through the flight paths you haven't found yet.

**Quest helpers**
- Accepts the quests Pathway suggests and turns in finished ones. Hold Shift to do it yourself.
- With several rewards, it highlights the best one (an upgrade, or else the most valuable) and can pick it for you.

**Zone guides**
Walk into a zone with enough to do and Pathway offers its main story, with how much of it you've done. Answer Always or Never here and it remembers. It only asks once you've stayed a few seconds, never while you're following a guide or route, never while flying, and not about the zone you land in.

**Find the nearest…**
Repair, food and drink, an innkeeper, your class trainer, any profession trainer, the bank, the auction house, a stable master or a spirit healer: the **Find** button on the map bar, or `/pw find repair`. The arrow takes you there, then back to your guide.

**How do I get that?**
Ctrl+Alt-click any item (a pet, a mount, gear, a curio) in chat, your bags, a vendor or the auction house, and Pathway shows where it comes from: the quest chain that rewards it, the nearest vendor that sells it, or where it drops, with a button to go there. Also `/pw get <item name>`.

**New quests, before the data has them**
When the game adds quests Pathway's data doesn't have yet, Pathway learns them as you play: who gives them and where, where the objectives are and which mobs count, and who takes them back. It works right away for all your characters. `/pw learned` shows what it's picked up, ready to send in for the next update. The journal's **Forever & secrets** tab lists Forever's own and hidden quests (like the chain to the Cozy Sleeping Bag) and anything learned.

**Leaving town**
As you leave a town or inn, Pathway asks if you want to do the quests still to pick up or turn in there first, and offers the nearest vendor when you're low on food and drink (or ammo, for hunters).

**Helpers**
- **Corpse run**: release and the arrow points to your body; once you're back on your feet, your guide carries on.
- **Repair reminder**: when your gear is nearly broken, offers the way to the nearest repair.
- **Full quest log**: offers to drop your gray quests in one click.
- **Gear upgrades** are weighed for your talents (a Holy paladin wants different stats than a Retribution one).

## Items

**Gear upgrades**
Pick up something better than what you're wearing and Pathway shows why (for example *+4 Strength, +1.6 DPS*) and asks whether to equip it. It uses class stat weights, skips what you can't use, and handles rings, trinkets and two-handers.

**Bags** (Lil's GoodBags, built in)
- One flat, quick window with a **Loot Inbox** for everything new.
- Categories, upgrade arrows, search tokens (`boe`, `upgrade`, `ilvl>20`).
- Smart junk selling, Session Yield, stack splitting and restock.
- **Bank Anywhere**: your bank from anywhere, with withdrawals queued for your next visit.

- Three layouts: **Grid**, **Categories** (with the Loot Inbox) and **Bags**: each bag as it really is. In Bags, click a bag's name to label it (Equipment, Consumables, Trade Goods, Quest, Recipes, Junk, Everything else), then **Clean up to labels** puts everything where it belongs.

## Players & PvP

- Hover any player for a card in their faction's colors (Alliance blue, Horde red): level, race and class, guild, talents, item level next to yours, PvP rank, honor kills, and your **odds in a fight**. It goes when you stop hovering.
- **Enemy healers** get a red cross over their heads, spotted from the heals they cast or their talents, even if you never click them.
- Target an enemy healer and you hear a ding (or any enemy player, or off).
- Talents and gear need you within inspect range for a moment. The odds are an estimate from level, gear, health, talents and class matchups.

## Minimap (Lil's GoodMap, built in)

Your zone map becomes your minimap, round or square.
- It follows you, and the real minimap sits inside it as a radar, so gathering nodes and tracking still show. Indoors the radar steps out, so the game's own building art never shows through, and what you track still shows at all times: inside the radar the game's own live blips, and everywhere it isn't (indoors, on a flight, past its edge) the herbs and ore you can gather (while you track them) and the town services you track, from Pathway's own data.
- **Terrain up close**: zoom in past the zone map's own detail and the game's real minimap terrain fades in under every pin: roads, houses, docks. It pans and zooms with the map, and your pins, arrow and route sit exactly on it. In towns and cities it zooms in to it by itself while you're there, and back out as you leave.
- Step into a cave, a building, a tunnel or onto another floor and it switches to that map when the game has one (and back when you leave). Indoors with no map of its own, like an inn, it fades over to the game's own picture of the rooms around you, with Pathway's pins on it.
- **Ride effects**: a rainbow tunnel on the Deeprun Tram, water on boats and ferries, wind on zeppelins, light on lifts, bubbles and an air gauge underwater, the spirit world as a ghost, and light spiralling in as you hearth. Boats and zeppelins show where they're going, the time left and a chime before you arrive. Each can be turned off, previewed, or calmed with Reduce motion.
- In a dungeon, raid or delve with a map it stays on and shows the whole floor you're on, with the game's own marker for you (add-ons aren't given your position in there). Where there's no map, the Blizzard minimap takes over.
- Your minimap buttons dock in a bar.
- Pathway's route is drawn right on it: a dotted line from you, with numbered stops.
- In combat its edge pulses red, and an amber glow warns you when an enemy is close enough to notice you (with a sound, if you like).
- Flight mode shows your route, a countdown and the landing time.
- Peek: hold a key to enlarge it.
- Group pings and markers, compass, zoom memory, and the edge arrow to your target.
- Farming loops through the spots you've gathered yourself.
- Shift-M shows or hides it.

## Fishing (Lil's Fishing Blinker, built in)

Cast Fishing once and fishing mode takes over:
- Scroll up to cast, scroll down to reel in.
- The screen clears and the splash gets loud.
- A bite timer, a fishing journal of your catches and spots, tackle in a click, and a fishing look on the minimap.
- Every action is one press from you.

## Light on your frame rate

Pathway only does work when something actually changes: a quest really comes or goes, you move, you open a window. Everything it shows can be switched off, and switching something off stops its work too. The performance meter (on the minimap bar) shows what each part costs, and its Run smoother panel turns any part off in a click.

## Settings

`/pw settings`: a **Home** page with the big switches (Bags, Minimap and Fishing can each be turned off completely) and the look, then a few short pages: Navigation, Quest help, Guides & map, Gear & bags, Bank, Minimap and Fishing. The minimap's many fine-tuning options and the finer bag details sit under **Advanced**, shown when you turn on *Show advanced settings*.

## Commands

| Command | |
|---|---|
| `/pw` | Go to the closest set of quests |
| `/pw pickup` · `/pw obj` · `/pw turnin` | Just one kind |
| The yellow **?** tab on the guide | Turn in every quest that's ready, in the quickest order |
| `/pw journal` | The quest journal |
| `/pw story` · `/pw zone` | This zone's storyline · all its quests, chain to chain |
| `/pw to westfall` | Go anywhere, the fastest way |
| `/pw flights` | Find flight paths you haven't discovered |
| `/pw find repair` | The nearest repair (or food, inn, class, bank, auction, mining…) |
| `/pw skip` · `/pw stop` | Skip a stop, or stop navigating |
| `/pw guide` | Show or hide the step guide window |
| `/pw gear` | Look through your bags for upgrades |
| `/gb` · `/gb bank` | Your bags · Bank Anywhere |
| `/goodmap` | Minimap menu |
| `/gmap check` | Try everything on the minimap and see what works |
| `/fb` | Fishing window |
| `/pw why 1234` | Why quest 1234 is or isn't suggested |
| `/pw settings` | Options |

## Install

Copy the `Pathway` folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` and restart the game or `/reload`. No other addons needed.
